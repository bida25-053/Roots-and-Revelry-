About the
Services

Roots and Revelry is excited to introduce our new eco-friendly landscaping services, designed for both residential and commercial properties across Botswana. Our approach combines creativity with sustainability to deliver landscapes that thrive naturally and stand the test of time.

"Sustainable landscaping solutions that enhance beauty while protecting the environment."

Our Services Include

We use native plants, efficient irrigation, and soil-friendly techniques to ensure long-term sustainability.

  • Garden design and planning
  • Irrigation system installation
  • Soil health assessment
  • Sustainable plant selection

Our Approach

Every project is designed to reduce water usage, support local biodiversity, and create outdoor spaces that are both visually appealing and environmentally responsible — now and for years to come.

Why Choose Us?

We combine creativity with sustainability to create landscapes that thrive naturally. Our approach reduces water usage, supports biodiversity, and ensures long-lasting results for every client.

Custom Solutions for Every Property

Whether redesigning a home garden or maintaining a large commercial green space, our expert team delivers customized solutions tailored to your specific needs, budget, and environmental goals.
