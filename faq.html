Your Minds

We've compiled the most common questions our clients ask about our services, community involvement, and expert tree care advice. If you don't find your answer here, reach out directly and we'll be happy to help.

What services does Roots and Revelry offer?

Roots and Revelry offers sustainable tree supply, eco-friendly landscaping, tree care guidance, environmental education, and community planting initiatives. We help individuals, families, schools, and organizations create greener, healthier spaces through expert support and quality plant care.

What is a Tree Preservation Order (TPO)?

A TPO protects trees that provide significant amenity benefit to an area. It is a criminal offence to cut down or prune these trees without written permission from the Local Planning Authority.

Do you provide tree care advice?

Absolutely. We provide practical care guides and expert advice on planting, watering, pruning, soil preparation, and long-term tree health. Our goal is to help every customer grow healthy, thriving trees with confidence.

Can schools or organizations partner with you?

Yes, we actively welcome partnerships with schools, community groups, environmental organizations, and businesses. We collaborate on tree planting events, educational workshops, sustainability projects, and awareness campaigns.

Do you offer landscaping services?

Yes, we provide eco-friendly landscaping support focused on sustainable design, tree selection, planting layouts, and green space enhancement for homes, schools, and community areas.

How can I get involved in community projects?

You can get involved by joining our tree planting events, volunteering in community greening projects, partnering with us, or supporting our environmental initiatives through collaboration and outreach.
