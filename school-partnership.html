About the
Programme

Roots and Revelry is proud to announce our partnership with local schools to promote environmental awareness and sustainability among young learners. This initiative empowers students with knowledge and practical skills in conservation.

"Inspiring the next generation through hands-on environmental education and tree planting."

Environmental Awareness

The programme introduces students to key topics such as climate change, biodiversity, and sustainable living. Through interactive lessons and outdoor activities, learners gain a deeper understanding of how their actions impact the environment.

Hands-On Planting

As part of the partnership, schools participate in tree planting workshops where students actively plant and care for trees within their school grounds — encouraging responsibility and long-term environmental stewardship.

What the Programme Includes

The initiative offers sustainability lessons, school garden development, and ongoing support for eco-friendly practices. Our team works closely with educators to ensure the programme is engaging, educational, and impactful.

Building a Greener Future

By working with schools, Roots and Revelry aims to cultivate a culture of environmental responsibility from a young age — contributing to a more sustainable future for communities across Botswana.
