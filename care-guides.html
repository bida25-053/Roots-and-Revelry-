How to Care for Newly Planted Trees

First year is critical — roots, mulching, watering
▼
  • Water deeply 1–2 times per week for the first year
  • Apply a 5–8cm layer of mulch around the base
  • Protect from strong winds and extreme heat
  • Inspect regularly for pests or signs of disease

Seasonal Tree Maintenance

Spring through winter care schedule
▼
  • Spring: Fertilize and check for new growth
  • Summer: Monitor watering and pest activity
  • Autumn: Remove fallen leaves and prune lightly
  • Winter: Protect young trees from frost damage
🪴

Indoor Plant Care Basics

Light, soil, watering, and rotation
▼
  • Provide adequate natural or indirect light
  • Use well-draining potting soil
  • Avoid overwatering — check soil moisture first
  • Rotate plants regularly for even growth

Fruit Tree Care Tips

Pruning, feeding, pollination, harvesting
▼
  • Prune annually to improve air circulation
  • Fertilize during the growing season
  • Ensure proper spacing between trees
  • Harvest fruit promptly when ripe

Pro Tips

Expert Tips for Thriving Trees

Practical advice from our nursery specialists to help every tree reach its full potential.

01

Watering Right

Deep, infrequent watering encourages roots to grow deeper — producing stronger, more drought-resistant trees.

  • Water at the base, not on leaves
  • Morning watering reduces evaporation
  • Use a slow drip for best results
02

Mulching Matters

A proper mulch layer is one of the most impactful things you can do for a newly planted tree's survival.

  • Apply 5–8cm depth around the base
  • Keep mulch away from the trunk
  • Replenish annually in spring
03

Pruning Principles

Proper pruning shapes growth, improves air circulation, and removes diseased or damaged branches before they spread.

  • Always use clean, sharp tools
  • Prune in late winter or early spring
  • Never remove more than 25% at once
04

Pest Prevention

Early detection prevents small pest problems from becoming devastating infestations that damage entire trees.

  • Inspect leaves weekly in summer
  • Use organic sprays where possible
  • Remove affected branches promptly
05

Fertilising Wisely

Over-fertilising can burn roots and cause excessive leafy growth at the expense of fruit or flowers.

  • Use slow-release fertiliser in spring
  • Avoid fertilising in winter months
  • Test your soil before applying
06

Seasonal Adjustments

Adapt your care routine to Botswana's distinct seasonal shifts — your trees will reward you with stronger growth.

  • Reduce watering in cool dry months
  • Increase feeding in the warm wet season
  • Shelter young trees from cold nights
