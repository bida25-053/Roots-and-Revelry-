Spring Tree Planting Event Success

February 20, 2026 Gaborone Community Park Roots & Revelry Team

Roots and Revelry proudly celebrates the success of our Spring Tree Planting Event, where over 500 native trees were planted to support biodiversity and create greener public spaces across Gaborone.

Over 120 volunteers joined hands to plant trees and support environmental sustainability — making this one of the most successful community events in our history.
500+Trees Planted
120+Volunteers
1 DayOf Action

The event brought together students, local residents, environmental groups, and community leaders. Participants worked collaboratively, planting indigenous trees carefully selected for their resilience and long-term environmental benefits across Gaborone's public green spaces.

Volunteers at Gaborone Community Park — February 20, 2026

In addition to planting, attendees learned about proper tree care, soil health, and the critical role trees play in improving air quality, preventing soil erosion, and supporting wildlife habitats. Our nursery specialists ran hands-on workshops throughout the day.

Community Impact

This initiative contributes to long-term environmental sustainability and strengthens community involvement in conservation efforts. It reflects our commitment to building healthier ecosystems and greener cities across Botswana.

Looking Ahead

Roots and Revelry plans to host more planting events and environmental programmes throughout the year across all three nursery locations in Gaborone, Francistown, and Maun. We invite everyone to take part and make a lasting impact.
